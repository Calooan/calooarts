Character Design

Bob, the main character is a fish tank head guy who lives in a big slanted city. He is a regular, even dull employee. Like most people in this city, he has almost the same daily routine. He is introverted and timid, cowardly, and afraid of things as if everything is not going his way. His work is clean up the dead fish on the street, but everything changes after he found an alive one.

Der, the fish's name. He is an abandoned little fish, because no one cares about fish's life in the city, except Bob. So he is puny and helpless, sensitive but still full of curious to the world.
